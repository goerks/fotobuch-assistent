What the app does

Preparing a photo book is tedious. The assistant does the groundwork and leaves the choices to you.

🗓

Events by time and place

From capture time and GPS the app forms days, outings and occasions. You rename, split or merge them.

🧹

Spots duplicates and bursts

Exact copies and near-identical shots are grouped. The sharpest, best picture is suggested.

📱

Screenshots and chat copies out

Screenshots and messenger copies never reach the book. Every decision can be changed.

🔢

A page counter that does the math

You set a range, for example 2 to 5 photos per page. Small events get one page, big ones several, ideally a spread. The counter shows the page count live.

🧩

Layouts per page

Portrait and landscape photos are mixed to fit. Pick a template per page, including full-bleed.

🌄

Panoramas across the spread

Very wide photos span both pages or get a page of their own. The page count stays consistent.

🎨

Six design themes

Cover, background, frames and typeface match. Title and subtitle are filled in from date and event.

🖨

Print-ready pages

Export as JPEG and PDF at 300 dpi, plus a page list. Reads photos from iPhone (HEIC) and cameras.

Step by step to your album

Seven guided steps. Go back any time; everything you decided is saved.

  1. 1

    Load photos

    Pick a folder, done. The app reads capture time, place and format straight from the files. Your originals are only read, never changed.

  2. 2

    Check events

    The app splits the trip into events by gaps and places. A slider sets the granularity and the timeline lets you split and merge events. Each event shows which photos make it into the book.

  3. 3

    Cull

    Bursts and duplicates are already grouped and the best photo is preselected. A burst strip shows all series with progress, and bars for sharpness and resolution explain the suggestion.

  4. 4

    Set the scope

    Choose a range per page and a density per event, or set a page count. A gallery per event shows which photos made it into the book and lets you override.

  5. 5

    Design

    Choose a theme, enter a title, pick the cover photo. The preview shows the cover and first pages right away.

  6. 6

    Flip through the book

    Spread by spread, like the printed book. The page strip shows every page: move them, insert blank pages, reorder pages or whole events.

  7. 7

    Export

    One click makes finished pages as JPEG and PDF plus a page list. Upload the photos or hand over the pages.

Download

Free, portable, no installation. Unpack or launch, done.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

Download (.exe)
  • Download the file and double-click it.
  • If Windows shows “protected your PC”, click “More info” and “Run anyway”. The app is not signed yet.

Mac

macOS, Apple silicon (M1 and newer)

Download (.zip)
  • Open the ZIP and drag the app to Applications.
  • First launch: right-click, “Open”, confirm. The app is not signed by Apple yet.

Linux

64-bit (x86_64), WebKitGTK 4.1

Download (.tar.gz)
  • Unpack the archive and run “fotobuch-assistent”.
  • Needs WebKitGTK 4.1, present on most desktops (Debian/Ubuntu: libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0).

To be honest

  • The app prepares the book. Importing directly into Cewe, Pixum or Ifolor software is not possible yet; you upload the finished pages there.
  • Print-service page rules (minimum pages, step size) are assumptions and can be adjusted. Check them with your provider.
  • The interface is in German. There is no English interface yet.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The app works entirely on your computer and needs no internet connection. There is no account and no cloud.

Does the app change my originals?

No. Photos are only read. Your decisions live in a “.fotobuch” subfolder that you can delete at any time.

Does it run on an older PC?

Yes, the app is built for modest computers. Thumbnails are computed once and then cached.

Which photo formats are read?

JPEG, PNG and iPhone HEIC. Capture time and GPS are read when stored in the file. Photos without a time appear as their own event at the end.

What does it cost?

Downloading and using it is free.

Can I order the book directly?

Not yet. You export finished pages, or numbered photos with a page list, and upload them to your print service.
